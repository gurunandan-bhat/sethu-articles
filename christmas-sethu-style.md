by Rebecca Manari

Big holidays like Christmas can be a stressful time for families with young children who’re sensitive to lights, loud party revellers and too many relatives fussing over them. So in true Sethu style, we decided to help the children through it all.

Our gentle, child-friendly Christmas party was a prelude to a fun and festive experience. We had sensory games like playing with faux snow and decorating a Christmas tree, and having a blast getting our hands messy while decorating cookies. The kids showcased their creativity by making their own Christmas ornament with their mums and dads. We were thrilled to even have a grandparent join us!

A little quiet time and cosy togetherness with a Christmas story culminated in devouring delicious Christmas goodies and snacks from home. And with Christmas being the season for giving, every child took home a Christmas goodie box and a gift!
