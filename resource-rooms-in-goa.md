Resource rooms are classrooms managed by special educators to support disabled students in mainstream schools. In Goa, they’re of two kinds:

- **Life skills resource rooms** enable children with moderate to severe intellectual disabilities to attend mainstream school even when they can’t follow the same curriculum as their peers.
- **Academic skills resource rooms** enable disabled children with mild to moderate support needs to participate in mainstream education by providing remedial support or modified curricula.
