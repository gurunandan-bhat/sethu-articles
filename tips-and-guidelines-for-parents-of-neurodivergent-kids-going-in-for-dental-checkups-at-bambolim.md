A tooth abscess is lots of fun to treat, said no one ever. So when Ryan\*, 5, went in to Dental Hospital, Bambolim, to have his abscess cleaned, his mum and he were in for a surprise. The staff and doctors at the hospital were polite and welcoming when they learnt that Ryan is autistic. He was led to a child-friendly paediatric ward and a special, fully air-conditioned kids room filled with toys and a library, designed in collaboration with Sanjay Centre for Special Education for disabled children. His parents were delighted to see him so happy and cooperative – the doctor even gave him three stars for being such a good little patient!

Two years of therapy has made a big difference to Ryan’s ability to cope outdoors. Things no longer feel as overwhelming and he’s learning to respond in socially appropriate ways.

Here are his mum’s best tips and guidelines for parents of neurodivergent kids going in for dental checkups at Bambolim.

\*Name changed

Procedure

1\. Registration: This costs Rs 100. Please carry your child’s Aadhaar card.

2\. Examination and history taking are conducted.

3\. Case papers are created.

3\. The child is taken to the paediatric ward in the new wing.

4\. Hand in your papers and await your turn.

5\. After another examination and an x-ray scan(at the old building) if necessary, the child is taken in for treatment.

Guidelines

1\. Registrations open at 9 am. The earlier you go, the better.

2\. There could be a long wait so carry snacks for your child.

3\. Prepare your child in advance for the experience.

4\. Be on time especially for the follow-ups. The staff and doctors are very punctual and adhere to the time allotted.
