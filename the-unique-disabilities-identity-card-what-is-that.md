A Unique Disability Identity Card is used all over India as an identity card for people with disability.

Where to apply for it?  
One can apply for this card from the comfort of your home, online, simply log onto: [www.swavlambancard.gov.in](http://www.swavlambancard.gov.in/)

What do I need before I apply?  
To apply for the UDID card, one first needs to have all the following documents saved as files uploaded onto your phone or computer:

**1. A scanned recent photograph of the applicant**  
**2. Address proof**  
**3. Identity proof  
4\. The applicant’s disability certificate  
5\. Scanned signature or thumbprint of the applicant**

How much does it cost?  
The UDID card is free of charge.

What are the benefits:  
The card can be used as an identity card  
It is proof of disability  
It eliminates the need to carry around many documents as it is an all in one, pocket-sized card.  
Helps the applicant to avail all the schemes that are available for persons of disability

Is it essential?  
No, this is not an absolutely essential document. However, if your child has challenges at school, with travel, medical problems, or financial difficulties, the card may help you to get several concessions to make life much easier for you and your family.
