<figure class="wp-block-image size-large">![](https://i0.wp.com/sethu.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/autism-tele-copy-1024x1013.jpg?resize=1024%2C1013)</figure>“My name is Amy and I live with my husband and son, Jack in the UK. It has been almost a year now that he is on the waiting list for his autism assessment here. We were offered speech therapy 6 months after informing them of Jack’s developmental delays. The sessions were for only 20 mins over video calling. It would go by so quickly that it would not be been possible for me to digest what they were saying, it wasn’t really helpful.

I had heard about Sethu and saw they were conducting sessions online. So we started autism intervention sessions with Sethu, Andre explained things to me so specifically, I do not know where I would be today without this understanding. Teletherapy with Sethu has been very good and Andre explains everything so patiently so it is easily understandable. I find his methods so much easier for me to learn and then apply the techniques with Jack. Andre even does live coaching with us which is so helpful in learning techniques more effectively.

I understand my son more than ever now – how to support him and how and when I can use the strategies in daily routines and playtimes. Jack has started to point, he tries to say some words and vocalizes a lot. He interacts more with us now and has started to imitate us and give more eye contact. I am no longer stressed with regard to my son. I have a lot of reassurance and I have accepted him for who he is. I have no problem with him being different.”

– Amy, mum of Jack.
