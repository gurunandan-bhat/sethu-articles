Author: Andre Velho | May 31, 2021

<figure class="wp-block-image size-large">![](https://i0.wp.com/sethu.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/andre_testimonial-1-1004x1024.jpg?resize=1004%2C1024)</figure>My son now helps me to dry the clothes on the line!

Whenever I take the clothes that need to be dried, my son follows me and wants to help me by giving the clothes to me, so much so the other day he wanted to clip his socks to dry all by himself.

When he goes for his shower, I have taught him to put his dirty clothes in the laundry for washing. While bathing, he soaps himself and rubs his hair and body after soaping himself.

On behalf of my son, my husband and myself, it takes me great pleasure to personally thank you, Andre, for supporting, guiding and helping us every step of the way.

I still remember the first day at Sethu, when Delton was diagnosed with Autism – my world turned upside down, my heart broke to pieces. I was confused, worried and had no idea of what Autism was and what it meant to help a child who is Autistic.

Our sessions at Sethu have made a world of difference to our family and today when I see my boy, I think, without your help, we would not have made so much of progress.

Thank you Andre!

~ Ms Rodrigues, mother of Delton, who has autism
