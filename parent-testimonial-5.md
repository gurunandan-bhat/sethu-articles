\*\*\*

It’s very difficult as a parent to accept the fact that your child has a problem. But the problem has a solution.

We have to first accept the issue and then work on the solution.

I am happy to share this feedback about our journey at Sethu.

The day we became part of Sethu as parents, we were not so confident about the outcomes for our son. Over the last 5 years our son has been undergoing various therapies, we have seen an evident improvement in him. We have realised that not every child has the same methods of learning. The therapists at Sethu have enlightened us and taught us as parents to select and apply the appropriate methods for our son to learn things easily in A variety of areas like speech, social communication, academics and social behaviour.

Due to the pandemic, we could continue with our physical sessions and had to move on to online mode. We were worried how he would do but with the strong support of the techniques used by the therapists like presentations, video demonstrations, sharing visuals, regular meet details and milestones, it was possible to keep the flow of sessions persistent and equally effective.

Thanks to your wonderful team for all your dedicated efforts. Special thanks to Andre Sir, Rani Ma’am, Anjali Ma’am and Mahera Ma’am for your contribution to our son’s development and continuous improvement.

Regards,  
Mr And Mrs V G
