Sethu’s Culture of Trust prioritises maintaining a safe and secure environment for the families and children we serve. Given the sensitivities surrounding our work, we recognise that maintaining complete confidentiality is essential for maintaining the standards of our services and optimising therapeutic outcomes.

 Here’s how we ensure confidentiality:

- All information about children and their families is kept strictly private.
- Information is only shared with other family members, schools or other parties with the express, written permission of the child’s parents or guardians.
- Within the team, details are only shared with colleagues who require the information for professional reasons.
- Information is never shared with press or media.
