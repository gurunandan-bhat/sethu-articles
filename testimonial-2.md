Author: Madhura Joshi | July 16, 2022

<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-full">![](https://i0.wp.com/sethu.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/294037362_5185725434809603_9219148613859336507_n-1.jpg?resize=843%2C863)</figure> Our son is on the Autism spectrum, although high functioning. He has suffered from anxiety, social challenges and other issues. We were put in touch with Ms Madhura Joshi at Sethu, when he was around 14 years old.

Ms Joshi studied his case, interviewed parents and the elder sister to get the holistic understanding of his issues. She gave him a good clarity about his condition, counselled, comforted and helped our son work through many of his worries &amp; challenges, through her regular (online) sessions with him. In addition, her patient and thorough explanations of his condition to us, his parents, along with reference educational material she provided, helped a lot! She also helped us understand the simple accommodations, we as family could make, to ease the discomfort caused by aspects of his condition.

Ms. Joshi’s unflagging patience, wonderful professionalism and clear dedication to assisting our son, has led to tremendous improvements in all areas of his life, over these past couple of years – although her sessions ended after some months.

Now 17, he has grown by leaps and bounds, in both academic &amp; physical achievements, thanks to his own dedication &amp; discipline that turned his life around in all spheres.

We credit all of this in a large part to Ms Joshi’s work with our son and her guidance to us as a family, to support him in the ways that mattered. Our sincere thanks to her &amp; to the Sethu team, for helping him transform and grow into the impressive young adult he’s growing into.
